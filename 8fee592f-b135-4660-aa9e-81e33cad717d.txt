Dear Tim,

The tide has come to carry me off from
the beach of bachelorism.

Such a voyage demands precision in
assembling a bridge crew.

In this pursuit, it was keenly important
to me that I reserve such intimate
posts for my brethren alone.

Indeed, not even the man whose wedding I
was in, and who introduced me to Ensign Yee
in the first place, has been enlisted to
this end.

But you, Sir, are no mere friend.
For almost as far back as I can remember,
you have been a part of my life. You are
unmatched in gentility, have been invincibly
loyal, and relentlessly humble.
It is for these qualities that I humbly
request the honor of having you at my
side on my personal Away Team.

Timothy Michael-Barret Santomango,
will ye ship with me on November 6th, 2027
as my Executive Officer?
