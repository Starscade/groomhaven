Dearest Finn,

The tide has come to carry me off from
the beach of bachelorism.

Such a voyage merits the most ancient
of ceremonies, and that ceremony is
most happily performed in the company
of Man's most intimate comrades.

It was never a question in my mind who,
if it ever became my decision to make,
I would approach to enlist for this occasion.

Yet, I assume nothing.
You are under no obligation to accept.
And should you choose to decline,
you will be no less welcome as a guest
at our celebration.

Finnian Zane Sheehan,
will ye ship with me on November 6th, 2027
as my Second Mate?
